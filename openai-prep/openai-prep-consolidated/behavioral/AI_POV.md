# Your point of view on AI

OpenAI's behavioral round spends serious time on your view of AI itself. This isn't "I love AI" territory. Interviewers listen for a real, defensible take.

## How Claude Code uses this file

When you trigger **`behavioral practice`** and pick "AI POV", Claude Code reads this and pushes back on your views the way an interviewer would. If empty, it helps you develop them.

## Anti-patterns

- "AI is so exciting" — substanceless
- "I think LLMs will change everything" — vague, true of any technology
- "Safety is important" — agreed by everyone, distinguishes nobody
- Reciting OpenAI's mission statement back to them — they wrote it
- Pretending to have read every paper — they'll catch it

The right shape: specific, defensible, connected to your actual engineering instincts, distinct from generic "AI is great" talk.
