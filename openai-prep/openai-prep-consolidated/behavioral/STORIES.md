# Behavioral STAR Stories

Where you write your STAR stories for the OpenAI behavioral round.

## How Claude Code uses this file

When you trigger **`behavioral practice`**, Claude Code reads this file and drills you on whichever story you pick. If a story is empty or has TODOs, it'll help you surface and draft it.

---

## The four stories OpenAI is likely to probe

The OpenAI behavioral round usually picks ONE story and drills it for 10+ minutes. Have these four ready:

### Story 1: Conflict with a peer engineer

**S — Situation:** _Context. Project, your role, who the peer was._
**T — Task:** _What needed to happen._
**A — Action:** _Specifically what YOU did. Names, conversations, decisions._
**R — Result:** _Outcome with numbers if possible. What you learned._

**What you'd do differently:** _Be specific._

## Quality check

For each story, you should be able to answer fast and specifically:
- Who else was involved? (names or roles)
- What were the trade-offs you weighed?
- What was the actual numerical outcome?
- What did you personally do vs what the team did?
- What did you consider but reject?
- What would you do differently today?

Vague answers ("we improved things," "the team came together") fail this round.
